Viral Clips Bot

A personal tool that posts League of Legends clips from Twitch as YouTube Shorts.

The bot is run by one channel owner, for their own YouTube channel only. It is not a public service, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it does

  • Finds popular League of Legends clips on Twitch.
  • Turns each one into a vertical Short with captions.
  • Uploads it to the owner's YouTube channel.
  • Reads the channel's own video statistics to learn which clips, titles and formats do best.
