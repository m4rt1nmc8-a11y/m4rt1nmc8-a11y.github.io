Privacy Policy

Viral Clips Bot · Last updated 22 October 2026

Viral Clips Bot is a personal tool. Its owner runs it for their own YouTube channel only. Nobody else signs in to it, and it collects no data about anyone else.

What Google data it uses

When the owner signs in with Google, the bot asks for three permissions:

  • Upload videos — to post Shorts to the owner's channel.
  • View the YouTube account — to check that uploaded videos still exist and are processed.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports — to read views, watch time, likes, shares and subscribers gained for the channel's own videos.

It uses this data only to upload clips and to learn which clips, titles, formats and upload times work best on the channel. Nothing else.

What it stores

  • For each uploaded video: its YouTube video ID, title, upload time and its performance numbers. These are kept in the owner's private database.
  • The Google sign-in token is kept as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository. The bot never sees or stores a password.

The data is kept while the bot is running and is deleted when the owner shuts it down.

Sharing

Google data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone. The only place it goes is the owner's own private Discord channel, where the bot posts upload alerts and a daily summary of how the videos are doing.

To write titles, the bot sends an AI service the clip's public Twitch details and a short list of hashtags. It never sends the AI your Google account data or analytics.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Viral Clips Bot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The bot uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's Privacy Policy applies to data Google handles.

Removing access

You can take away the bot's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once access is removed, the bot can no longer upload or read anything.
